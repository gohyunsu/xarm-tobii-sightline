Review method

Search outward from the causal claim

The map prioritizes primary publications, author project pages, official documentation, and current neighboring work. It does not treat a demo video, arXiv preprint, peer-reviewed experiment, and manufacturer page as equivalent evidence.

Question decomposition

Search separately for multiview cost, adaptive viewpoint, gaze intention/control, passive visual behavior, and platform constraints.

Setting extraction

Record physical versus simulated robot, task, camera arrangement, participants, gaze role, outcome, and comparator.

Role assignment

Label each source as competitor, baseline, foundation, or boundary. One paper can influence design without validating the proposal.

Four lineages

Similar components, different scientific questions

The role assigned to gaze and the degree of camera automation distinguish neighboring work more clearly than hardware names.

A · direct neighbor

Gaze → intent or control

Gaze helps infer a goal, select a target, command a camera, or support shared autonomy. This is active prior art, so Sightline does not claim gaze-to-target control as novel.

B · measurement foundation

Gaze → behavior or workload

Fixations, AOI dwell, transitions, and scanpaths explain visual search and operating strategy. This family supports measurement validity, not the intervention effect.

C · strong baseline

Geometry → viewpoint

Visibility and occlusion can optimize camera pose or feed selection. This family requires a geometry-only C2 condition.

D · experimental foundation

Layout → human factors

Single, multiple, manual, and autonomous camera modes affect success, visual switching, workload, and usability. “More cameras” is already testable prior work.

Original synthesis. Sightline sits between passive gaze analysis and direct gaze control, but its actual contribution depends on C3 outperforming geometry-only C2.

Evidence discipline

Four rules for figures, citations, and claims

Visual similarity between a prior demo and the proposed system does not transfer performance. Sources are displayed with their role and limitation.

Mark prior results

Every external figure states “prior work” or “official media” and “not our result.”

Carry the setting

Simulation/physical status, task, camera configuration, participant sample, and robot constrain generalization.

Prefer the original record

Link the publication, author project, official manufacturer documentation, or current preprint—not a search snippet.

Use the strongest baseline

Do not omit persistent multiview or geometry-only adaptation merely because a single-view baseline produces a larger effect.
